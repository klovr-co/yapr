# Twitch Streamer Interview Questions

Tiered questions for building `twitch-context.md` progressively.

## Tier 1 — Essential (ask on first use, any copy type)

1. **What's your channel about?** (niche + vibe in their own words)
2. **How would you describe your on-stream personality?** (e.g. "chill and educational", "chaotic energy", "hype but wholesome", "dry humor")
3. **Who's your target viewer?** (e.g. "developers who want to learn", "casual FPS players 18-25", "anyone who likes horror games")

Save these immediately, then proceed with the copy request.

## Tier 2 — Community (ask when writing panels, raid messages, or sub celebrations)

- Do you have a community name or nickname for your viewers?
- Any recurring in-jokes, memes, or catchphrases your community uses?
- What's your channel name / handle?

## Tier 3 — Format & Schedule (ask when writing schedule panels or stream descriptions)

- How often do you stream and what days/times?
- Do you have recurring show formats or segment names? (e.g. "Midnight Mondays", "Sunday Chill Sessions")
- What categories/games do you play most?

## Tier 4 — Goals & Promotion (ask when writing social posts or off-platform copy)

- What platforms do you promote your stream on? (Twitter/X, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube)
- What's your current follower/subscriber count range? (helps calibrate social proof framing)
- What's your primary growth goal right now? (more followers, more subs, bigger community, brand deals)

## Tier 5 — Tone Nuance (ask when copy feels generic or misaligned)

- Give me 3 adjectives that describe how you want your channel to feel.
- Is there a streamer whose energy or brand you admire? (for reference, not copying)
- What should your copy NEVER sound like? (e.g. "not corporate", "not cringe hype", "not too formal")

## Profile Update Rule

After each tier question is answered, append the answer to `.claude/twitch-context.md` under the appropriate section. Never re-ask questions already in the profile.
